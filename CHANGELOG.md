# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

First public release.

- Web remote for iPhone and Android: Classic and Apple TV layouts, clickpad, touchpad for TV browsers, live keyboard, app switcher, HDMI inputs, volume, power, favourites, second device
- Settings page `/settings`: devices and IPs with network search, language, layout, favourites and order, touchpad speed, vibration, token, ready addresses for Siri, Home Assistant, Alexa and Google
- AirPlay with AirPlay Server, optional CleanYourTV launcher
- Setup prompt for AI agents in English and German, with progress tracking and step checks
- Runs in Docker, as a systemd service or on a PC; demo mode
- Tools: find-tv, tv-info, test-remote, airplay-setup, check-readme
- Docs and examples in English and German
