# Code of conduct

This project is a friendly place for people who want a better TV. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background or language.

**Please:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, help beginners, give feedback on the work and not the person, and keep IPs, tokens and personal data out of public posts.

**Not okay:** insults, harassment, discrimination, sharing someone's private information, spam or advertising, and pushing others to take risks with their devices.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing it. Report problems privately through GitHub (Security tab → Report a vulnerability, or contact the maintainer listed on the profile).

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
